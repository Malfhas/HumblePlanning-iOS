# Privacy policy: Humble Planning for iOS

**Publisher:** [Indumia](https://indumia.com/) · **Contact:** contact@indumia.com

Humble Planning for iOS is a client for a Humble Planning server, usually run by your employer or organisation. That organisation decides what the server stores and is responsible for it. Its own privacy policy applies to your account and your planning data.

## What the app handles

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| Sign-in identifier and password | Signing in | Only to the server address you entered. Never stored on the device (unless you save it in the Passwords app) |
| Session of your account | Staying signed in | In the Keychain of the device, sent only to that server |
| Planning data, profile, attachments, conversations with the assistant | The app's purpose | Between the device and your server. A copy of your own tasks is kept encrypted on the device for offline reading |
| Device model, iOS and app versions, language | Listing your signed-in devices, keeping the app compatible | To your server |
| Push token of the device | Real-time alerts | To your server and, to deliver a push, to the Apple Push Notification service through the publisher's push relay, which stores nothing |

A push only holds an alert identifier: the content of alerts is read from your server, not sent through Apple or the relay.

The publisher runs no server receiving your planning data and uses no advertising and no analytics. The AI assistant is optional and provided by your server: your questions go to your server, which sends them to the AI provider its administrator chose.

## Permissions

| Permission | Use |
|---|---|
| Camera | Scanning the sign-in QR code, only when you open the scanner. Images are neither stored nor sent |
| Notifications | Showing alerts and reminders |
| Photos and files | Only the photos and files you choose to attach to a task |

## Your choices

Alerts can be turned off in the app's settings or in your web profile. Signing out deletes the data kept on the device, as does deleting the app. To access or delete your account data, contact your organisation's administrator of the server.

## Security

The connection to the server uses HTTPS. Data kept on the device is encrypted with a key held in its Keychain, readable on this device only, and excluded from backups.
